# attention

Run `node scripts/royalties.mjs attention --json`. Group by category: statements overdue or due soon, payments not made, advances due, manuscripts late, contracts not signed, legal deposit, rights income overdue, licences expiring, reversions, missing royalty rates, tax details. Lead with anything that breaks a contract or the law (an overdue statement, a missed deposit, a missing GST agreement), then money owed to authors, then the rest. One line each, with the record reference.
