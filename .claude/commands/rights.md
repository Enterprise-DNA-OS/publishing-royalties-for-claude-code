# rights

Run `node scripts/royalties.mjs rights --json`. For income: record money received with `node scripts/royalties.mjs receive <income ref> [--date= --amount=]` once the operator confirms it is in the bank. Received income flows to authors on the next royalty run at the share in their contract.
